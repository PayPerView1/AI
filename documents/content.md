# Content Submission & Review

Once a Clipper is accepted into a campaign, they must follow the content submission and review workflow to receive compensation.

## Step 1: Draft Creation & Upload
- Clippers record and edit the short-form video according to the campaign brief.
- The raw or watermarked video draft must be uploaded to the platform dashboard for brand review before posting publicly.

## Step 2: Brand Approval
- Brands have 48 hours to approve the draft or request revisions (limit of 2 revision requests per contract).
- If the brand does not take action within 48 hours, the draft is auto-approved to prevent delays.

## Step 3: Public Posting & Link Submission
- Once approved, the Clipper posts the video to their linked social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube).
- The Clipper must submit the public link of the post to the platform within 24 hours of posting.
- System starts tracking analytics 2 hours after link submission.
