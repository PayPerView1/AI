# Moderation, Guidelines & Appeals

To maintain a healthy ecosystem, we enforce strict trust and safety policies.

## Content Guidelines
All submitted videos must comply with our advertising guidelines:
- No hate speech, explicit content, violence, or political campaigns.
- Must clearly disclose sponsored content (e.g., using #ad, #sponsored) in accordance with FTC guidelines.
- Video must not contain unauthorized copyright materials.

## Strike System
Violations of the platform guidelines result in account strikes:
- **Strike 1 (Warning):** Video deleted; earnings from the campaign forfeited.
- **Strike 2 (Temporary Suspension):** Account suspended for 14 days. Clipper cannot apply for new campaigns.
- **Strike 3 (Permanent Ban):** Wallet frozen; account closed.

## Appeals Flow
Users can appeal strikes or bans within 30 days. Appeals are reviewed by our trust team within 7 working days. Provide campaign ID and evidence to support your case.
