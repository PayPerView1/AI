# Campaigns

Campaigns are the primary mechanism for Brands to procure content from Clippers.

## Creating a Campaign (Brands)
1. **Define Brief:** Add title, objective, style guidelines, and mandatory hashtags/links.
2. **Select Campaign Type:**
   - **Pay-per-View (PPV):** Clippers are paid a fixed rate per 1,000 verified views (e.g., $5 CPM).
   - **Flat Rate:** Clippers are paid a fixed sum for creating and posting the video (e.g., $100 per video).
3. **Set Budget & Caps:** Define the maximum budget (e.g., $1,000 max) and maximum payout per clipper (e.g., $200 cap per clipper).
4. **Fund Campaign:** Escrow the budget using a credit card or wallet balance.

## Joining a Campaign (Clippers)
1. **Browse Campaigns:** Filters by niche, payment type, and minimal tier requirement.
2. **Apply:** Submit a short pitch or wait for instant approval if automatic matchmaking is enabled.
3. **Receive Asset Kit:** Once accepted, download raw video assets, logos, and promotional links provided by the brand.
