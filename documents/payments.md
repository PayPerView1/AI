# Payments & Billing

Brands must fund their accounts to cover campaign payouts. 

## Depositing Funds
- **Supported Payment Methods:** Major Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX) and ACH/Wire transfers.
- **Auto-Refill:** Brands can enable auto-refill to prevent campaign pauses when the budget gets low (triggers when balance falls below 15% of the active campaign budgets).
- **Invoices:** Invoices are generated automatically on the 1st of every month for the previous month's deposits and spending.

## Escrow System
- When a campaign is launched, the entire budget is placed in a secure **Escrow Wallet**.
- This ensures Clippers are guaranteed payment for approved work.
- If a campaign ends and the entire budget is not spent (e.g., PPV caps not reached), remaining funds are returned to the Brand's main balance.
