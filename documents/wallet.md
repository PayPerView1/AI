# Clipper Wallet & Withdrawals

All earnings from campaigns accumulate in the Clipper's personal platform wallet.

## Earnings Processing
- **Flat-Rate Campaigns:** Earnings are credited to the wallet 7 days after the Brand approves the submission.
- **Pay-per-View (PPV) Campaigns:** Earnings are calculated daily and credited 14 days after the view tracking window closes (or on the 30th day, whichever comes first).

## Withdrawals
- **Minimum Threshold:** The minimum withdrawal limit is $50.
- **Payout Methods:** Direct Bank Deposit (ACH via Stripe), PayPal, and Payoneer.
- **Processing Time:** Withdrawals are processed twice a week (Tuesdays and Fridays). It usually takes 1-3 business days to hit your bank account.
- **Fees:** Stripe transfers are free of charge. PayPal transactions incur a 2% fee up to $10 maximum.
