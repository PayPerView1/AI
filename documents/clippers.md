# Clipper Accounts

Clipper accounts are for short-form content creators (TikTokers, YouTubers, Instagram Reel creators) who want to monetize their video creation and editing skills.

## Getting Started
To apply for campaigns, Clippers must optimize their profile:
1. **Link Social Handles:** Connect TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram accounts via OAuth. This allows the platform to fetch and verify follower counts and historic views.
2. **Niche Selection:** Tag accounts with relevant niches (e.g., Tech, Beauty, Gaming, Finance).
3. **Portfolio Upload:** Show three examples of your best short-form work.

## Performance Metrics
- **Clipper Tier:** Creators are tiered (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) based on average views and adherence to guidelines.
- **Engagement Score:** Calculated from average likes, shares, and comments on submitted videos.
- **Completion Rate:** Percentage of accepted campaigns successfully delivered. Higher completion rates lead to recommendations to premium brands.
