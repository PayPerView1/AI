# Brand Accounts

Brand accounts are designed for companies, marketing agencies, and product owners who want to run short-form video campaigns.

## Account Setup & Verification
To start launching campaigns, Brands must complete company verification:
1. **Company Information:** Legal name, registry details, and website URL.
2. **Billing Profile:** Add a valid credit card or link a corporate bank account.
3. **VAT/Tax ID:** Required for brands operating within the EU or other taxable jurisdictions.

## Brand Dashboard Features
- **Campaign Manager:** Create, edit, and monitor short-form video campaigns.
- **Clipper Directory:** Browse portfolios, engagement scores, and review metrics of creators.
- **Analytics Suite:** Track aggregate views, conversions, and cost-per-view (CPV) across all active campaigns.
- **Budget Control:** Allocate funds to specific campaigns and pause budgets at any time.
