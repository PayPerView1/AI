# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Here are answers to the most common questions on the Brands & Clippers platform.

## General
### Q: What is the Brands & Clippers Platform?
A: It is a matching platform where Brands set up short-form video campaigns and Clippers create content to fulfill them for payment.

## For Brands
### Q: How do I create a campaign?
A: Navigate to the Campaign Manager on your Brand Dashboard. Click "Create Campaign", fill in the brief, choose your payout model (Pay-per-View or Flat Rate), fund the escrow budget, and launch.

### Q: What happens to unused campaign budgets?
A: Remaining funds in escrow are automatically returned to your Brand balance at the end of the campaign's tracking window.

## For Clippers
### Q: How do I withdraw my earnings?
A: Go to your Wallet, click "Withdraw", choose your preferred payment method (Bank Direct, PayPal, Payoneer), and confirm. Minimum amount is $50.

### Q: Why has my payment not cleared yet?
A: Earnings have processing wait times: 7 days for Flat-Rate campaigns and up to 30 days for PPV campaigns. Check the specific dates in your Wallet history.

### Q: Can I change my account type from Clipper to Brand?
A: No, account types are permanent. If you need to switch, you must create a new account using a different email address.
